01 / The approach

Make the website match the business.

A website can quietly become outdated while the business around it grows. We identify what is holding the current experience back, preserve valuable content and rebuild the parts that need a change.

We start by looking closely at your existing website: what is working, what is getting in the way and where visitors are dropping off. This gives us a clear picture of what should be kept, improved or rebuilt.

We reorganise the site around how your customers actually look for information. Clear navigation, logical page structures and stronger user journeys make the redesigned website easier to understand and easier to use.

We give the website a new visual direction that reflects the business you are today. We can modernise layouts, typography, imagery, calls to action and brand presentation while keeping the parts of your identity that customers recognise.

A redesign should feel better as well as look better. We improve page structure, assets and implementation to create a faster experience that is more reliable for visitors and easier to maintain.

We design and test the experience around smaller screens rather than simply shrinking the desktop version. Navigation, content hierarchy, buttons and forms are considered carefully for phones and tablets.

When the new site is ready, we plan the move carefully. Content, redirects, key settings and launch checks are handled so the transition is controlled and the new website is ready to perform from day one.

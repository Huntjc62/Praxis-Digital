01 / Before you build

Not every old website needs a new one.

A website can look dated and still do its job. A beautiful website can also fail to generate a single enquiry.

This quiz looks beyond appearance. It considers age, mobile experience, content management, enquiries, speed, integration, security and how closely your website still fits the business behind it.

There is no sales trap. Your result may be that you don't need a rebuild yet. If you do need work, we'll explain what kind of change makes sense.

03 / What your result means

Your website probably doesn't need replacing yet.

The fundamentals look healthy. Keep improving the content, conversion journey and performance rather than rebuilding for the sake of it.

Your website could benefit from a transformation.

The business has probably moved on faster than the website. A strategic redesign can improve messaging, UX, mobile experience and conversion without starting from zero.

It may be time to rebuild from the ground up.

Several core parts of the experience are holding the business back. A rebuild can create a stronger foundation for design, content, SEO, systems and future growth.

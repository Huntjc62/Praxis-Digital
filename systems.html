01 / The approach

Make work flow better.

We map the workflow, identify repetitive tasks and build a system that puts the information, actions and people together. The result is less admin and a clearer view of what is happening.

Bring enquiries, customer information and follow-up activity into one place. We can create a CRM experience that reflects your sales process instead of forcing your team into a generic workflow.

Make it easier for customers to enquire or book while reducing manual administration behind the scenes. Forms, availability, notifications and workflows can all be connected into one experience.

Give customers a secure place to access information, documents, updates or services. Portals can be tailored to the exact information and actions each customer needs.

Turn scattered business data into useful views of what is happening. We can build dashboards around the metrics your team actually needs to make decisions.

Identify repetitive tasks and connect the steps together. Automated notifications, status changes, data movement and routine processes can reduce admin and help your team spend more time on valuable work.

Different people need different levels of access. We can build permissions around roles, teams and responsibilities so users see the information and tools they need without exposing everything to everyone.
